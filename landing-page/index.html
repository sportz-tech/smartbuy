Affiliate Network & Publisher Policy Details

Note for Sovrn //Commerce, Skimlinks, and Affiliate Partners:

SmartBuy is a browser helper utility designed to provide price alerts, charts, and coupon automation to shoppers. The extension monetizes by generating affiliate sub-tracking parameters (VigLink/Skimlinks/Admitad API nodes) solely in response to direct user click interactions ("Activate Cashback" CTAs inside the side panel or floating pill).
